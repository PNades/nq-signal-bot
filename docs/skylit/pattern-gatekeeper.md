# Pattern: The Gatekeeper

## Definition

A high-value node that sits between price and further high-value nodes, preventing continuation. This node then serves as a key support or resistance level. It is a commonly seen heatmap setup.

## How to Approach

- Mark gatekeeper strikes on the chart. Price often stalls or reverses as it approaches them.
- A reversal at the gatekeeper is more likely to hold when the gatekeeper node's value is far larger than the nodes beyond it.
- Price action and market structure matter as much as the map.
