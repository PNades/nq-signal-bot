# Pattern: Trend

## Definition

Price fixates on a King Node far away from spot, with comparatively small counter-directional skew. As price trades toward the initial King Node, it then selects the node above it as the new King Node.

## Structure

- A King Node far away from spot  
- Comparatively small counter-directional skew

## Behavior

- Price trades toward the King Node.  
- As it approaches, the node above it becomes the new King Node.  
- Nodes away from price tend to fade in value.  
- Nodes in the direction of the trend increase in value.  
- Trend days tend to stair-step.

## If Price Trades Away From the King Node

Watch the values of the nodes around the King Node's strike.

| Node values around the King Node | What it suggests |
| :---- | :---- |
| Stay relatively unchanged or increase | Price may eventually trade back toward it |

## How to Approach

- Look for pullbacks as the place for the trend to resume, as long as price action and the heatmap still support it.  
- Do not chase price on a trend day. Chasing makes the risk harder to define.
