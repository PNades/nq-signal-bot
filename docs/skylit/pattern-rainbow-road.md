# Pattern: Rainbow Road

## Definition

Rainbow Road is a heatmap with multiple prominent nodes of both positive and negative values, often looking like a rainbow. It has no clear range for price to trade within, which leads to choppy and erratic price action. These are no-trade conditions.

## Structure

- Multiple prominent positive and negative nodes
- No clear directional bias
- No dominant nodes
- No defined ceiling or floor

## Behavior

- Random movement
- No clean reactions
- No structure to lean on
- Choppy and erratic price action

## How to Approach

- When positioning is this vague, the map offers little to lean on.
- Stand aside until a clearer structure forms.

## Rainbow Road vs. Whipsaw

| | Whipsaw | Rainbow Road |
|---|---|---|
| Ranges | More defined ranges | No defined ceiling or floor |
| Edge | Fade the extreme ends of ranges | None. No-trade conditions. |
