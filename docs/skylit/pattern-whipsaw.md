# Pattern: The Whipsaw

## Definition

A whipsaw is a trap environment caused by a lack of confluence across indices. The map is convoluted and the indices give conflicting signals, but ranges still exist. The only edge comes from fading the extreme ends of ranges.

## Structure

- Conflicting signals across indices (lack of confluence)
- Convoluted map
- Ranges still exist

**Illustration of conflicting signals:** SPX bullish, QQQ bearish, SPY pinned. It can be any variation. The key is a lack of confluence.

## Behavior

- Fake moves
- Rapid reversals
- Both sides get trapped

## How to Approach

- Treat it as a trap environment.
- The only edge is fading the extreme ends of ranges.
