# Pattern: Beach Ball (Overshoot Setup)

## Definition

A beach ball is when price overshoots a node, moving beyond it, and then reacts after punching through. It is not a breakout and not a clean rejection. It is an overshoot followed by a reaction and potential reversion.

## Structure

- Price overshoots a node

## Behavior

- Price moves beyond the node
- Then reacts after punching through

**Sequence:** Overshoot → reaction → potential reversion

## Key Doctrine

- We do NOT trade breakouts.
- A beach ball is not a breakout.
- A beach ball is not a clean rejection.

## Gamma Context

| Regime | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Positive gamma | Can occur |
| Negative gamma | More common |
