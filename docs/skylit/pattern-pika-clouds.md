# Pattern: Pika Clouds (Positive Gamma Clusters)

## Definition

Pika clouds are dense clusters of multiple positive gamma (yellow) nodes. They act like gravity wells. Price does not move cleanly through them.

## Structure

- Dense clusters of positive gamma nodes (multiple)

## Behavior

- Price slows
- Trend tends to halt
- Higher likelihood of price rejecting
- Price gets pinned
- Movement becomes inefficient
- Price sticks, rotates, and struggles inside the cloud

## Key Doctrine

Pika clouds are not bullish or bearish by default. What matters is magnitude:

| Node size | Influence |
|---|---|
| Larger nodes | Stronger pull |
| Smaller nodes | Weaker influence |

## Mental Model

A gravity well. Price does not move cleanly through it. It sticks, rotates, and struggles.
